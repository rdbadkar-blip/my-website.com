Is it free to use?

Making and downloading a site here costs nothing. Hosting and a custom domain name are separate and are bought from the host you choose.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You fill in simple boxes and the website is written for you.

Can I change my site later?

Yes. Open the builder again, change anything and download a new file. Your last draft is kept in this browser.

Who owns the website?

You do. The file is yours to keep, move or edit.
